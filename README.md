# I don't watch too many shows, but when I did..

**Video Game High School was a cultural rediscovery!**

>   It wasn't some high-budget series on Netflix, HBO, or any streaming services even. It's on Youtube, one of the most relevant media-sharing platform.
>   Though being a public platform means mediocrity is guaranteed, VGHS is a seemingly-satire series of gamer culture.. (but it's more than that)
>   Long story short, it embraces being as terrible as those poorly-produced Western shows, but in the hands of some of the greatest editors that has encapsulated the true gamer experience, in HIGH SCHOOL!
>   It would've been much fun to talk about it as I show you the series itself. I'm not too good of a speaker to dedicate a well-written essay about it, because it's just too good for me to desecrate this relic of the past.
